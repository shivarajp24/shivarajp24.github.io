Think Before You Click: Avoid Phishing Scams

Phishing is one of the most common cyber threats targeting individuals and organizations worldwide. Stay vigilant to protect your sensitive information from malicious actors.

// WHAT IS IT?

Phishing is a fraudulent attempt to obtain sensitive information by disguising communication as trustworthy sources. Attackers use deceptive emails, texts, or websites to trick victims into revealing passwords, credit card numbers, or personal data.

// HOW IT WORKS

Attackers send mass messages containing malicious links or attachments designed to look legitimate. When a victim clicks the link, they are directed to a fake website that mimics a real service to steal credentials. Alternatively, downloading an attachment may install malware on the device to compromise data.

// REAL WORLD EXAMPLE

In 2020, attackers phished Twitter employees via a support tool to gain access to high-profile accounts, including those of Barack Obama and Elon Musk. They posted cryptocurrency scams that generated nearly $120,000 in donations before the accounts were secured.

// PROTECTION TIPS
  • Verify the sender's email address and look for subtle misspellings.
  • Hover over links to check the actual URL before clicking.
  • Enable multi-factor authentication on all your accounts.
  • Never download unexpected attachments from unknown sources.
  • Report suspicious messages to your IT security team immediately.
// DID YOU KNOW?

Over 90% of all cyberattacks start with a phishing email.
